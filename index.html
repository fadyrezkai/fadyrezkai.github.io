About

I am an ML researcher working on world models for embodied robotics at Samsung AI Research, Cambridge, where I build hierarchical planners that only commit to sub-goals the world model can actually reach. I recently submitted my PhD at the University of Edinburgh, advised by Timothy Hospedales and Henry Gouk.

My research asks whether our measurements of AI progress capture what they claim to. I showed that a learned optimizer’s reported gains depend on the evaluation protocol, built certified performance guarantees for few-shot transfer of foundation models (ICML 2026), and showed that reward-model accuracy doesn’t predict how the aligned model behaves for users (EMNLP 2026).

Before the PhD, I co-founded FastAutomate, a GUI-automation agent startup.

I am looking for research roles in evaluation, post-training and world models from March 2027.
