About

I work to bridge the gap between benchmark performance and real-world AI capabilities. My research tests whether our experiments support the claims we make about AI, and builds methods whose gains hold up, across evaluation, post-training and world models.

I'm currently working on world models at Samsung AI Research, Cambridge, and recently submitted my PhD at the University of Edinburgh, advised by Timothy Hospedales and Henry Gouk. Along the way I held an A*STAR PhD Research Fellowship working on Singapore's S$70M National Multimodal LLM Programme, interned at Samsung AI Research, and co-founded FastAutomate, a GUI-automation agent startup.

I'm looking for research roles in evaluation, post-training and world models from March 2027.
